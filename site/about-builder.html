SNN Raj Corp history: from SNN Builders to SNN Raj Corp

SNN Raj Corp was founded in 1994 and has built homes in Bengaluru since then. It was earlier known as SNN Builders, and many of its communities carry the SNN Raj name.

SNN Builders Private Limited was incorporated in 2009 as part of the SNN Group. By May 2016, the group had completed more than 40 projects with about 8.0 million sq ft of saleable area, largely residential apartments, mainly in South and South-East Bengaluru. Its homes were sold under the SNN Raj brand, among them SNN Raj Serenity, SNN Raj Grandeur, SNN Raj Greenbay, SNN Raj Etternia and SNN Raj Neeladri, a community of 103 apartments completed by April 2016.

SNN Kudlu continues that line in South-East Bengaluru. It is also known as SNN Raj Etternia Phase 2, and SNN Raj Etternia, on Haralur Road, is one of the developer's completed communities.

SNN Raj Corp leadership

The company is led by its two founders. Shah Sanjay is Founder and Managing Director, and Shivashankar Bhat is Founder and Director.

Knowing who runs a developer helps when the paperwork arrives. When bookings for SNN Kudlu open, check that the company named as developer in the agreement is the one you have read about here, and that every payment is made to the developer and not to anyone else.

Where SNN Raj Corp builds

SNN Raj Corp builds in Bengaluru, in two segments: apartments and villas. Its head office is at 4/A, 2nd floor, Elephant Rock Road, Jayanagar 3rd Block, Bengaluru 560011.

Its projects are spread across the city: Haralur Road, Begur, Electronic City, BTM Layout, JP Nagar, Bilekahalli and Bannerghatta Road in the south and south-east, Chandapura on the Hosur Road side, and Hebbal in the north. SNN Kudlu, between Haralur Road and Kudlu Gate, adds to its work in South-East Bengaluru.

What SNN Kudlu brings to the SNN Raj Corp portfolio

SNN Kudlu is planned on about 28.7 acres with 1,917 apartments in six towers of up to 24 upper floors, 60+ amenities, a clubhouse and about 10.9 acres of landscape beside a lake. Its homes run from the 2 BHK to two 4.5 BHK plans with four bedrooms and an extra room.

As of 17 October 2026, SNN Kudlu is in pre-launch, with prices on request. Prices, areas and the payment plan will be announced at launch.

Checking a developer before you book at SNN Kudlu

  • Visit a completed project, such as SNN Raj Etternia on Haralur Road, and look at how the buildings and common areas have aged.
  • Visit an ongoing site, such as Raj High Gardens, to see the developer's current construction.
  • Ask for the cost sheet, the payment plan and the agreement for the exact SNN Kudlu home before paying a token.
  • Make every payment to the developer; this website does not collect payments.
