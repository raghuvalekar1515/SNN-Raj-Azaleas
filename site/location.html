SNN Kudlu and the roads around it

Three useful reference points are Haralur Road, about 3.1 km from the site, Kudlu Gate on Hosur Road, about 4.4 km, and HSR Layout Sector 1, about 5.5 km. Knowing all three helps when you give directions or compare SNN Kudlu with other homes in the area.

Sitting between Haralur Road and Kudlu Gate, the site has the Haralur side and the Hosur Road side of South-East Bengaluru on either hand. That makes it workable for a household with one person heading towards Sarjapur Road or the Outer Ring Road and another towards Electronic City.

Living by the lake at SNN Kudlu

SNN Kudlu is planned beside a lake, with about 10.9 acres of landscape running down to the lake edge and the six towers set around a central landscape. Hosa Lake, listed among the nearby landmarks, is about 3.5 km from the site.

Lake views are part of the appeal, but they depend on where a home sits. When you visit, see the lake side of the site, and when the floor plans are out, ask which towers and floors face the water.
