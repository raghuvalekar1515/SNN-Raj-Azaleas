SNN Kudlu Location

SNN Kudlu is off Hosa Road near Silver County Road, between Haralur Road and Kudlu Gate in South-East Bengaluru, close to HSR Layout. Haralur Road is about 3.1 km away, Kudlu Gate about 4.4 km, the Hosa Road metro station about 5.4 km and Silk Board Junction about 7.9 km, with Sarjapur Road, Electronic City and the Outer Ring Road within easy reach.
