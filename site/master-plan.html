SNN Kudlu Master Plan

The master plan sets six towers around a central landscape on a lakeside site of about 28.7 acres. Towers A to D rise to two basements, ground and 24 floors, and Towers E and F to ground and 22. Internal roads, basement access and a fire driveway circle the community, with an arrival court and drop-off, the clubhouse, parks and open spaces, civic amenity land and about 10.9 acres of landscape running down to the lake edge.

How to read the SNN Kudlu master plan

A master plan is the bird's-eye view of a whole community. It shows where each tower stands, how cars and people enter and move through the site, and how much ground is left open. Read it before the floor plans: it tells you what your home will look out on and how far you will walk to the clubhouse, the lawns and the gate.

On the SNN Kudlu plan, start at the arrival court and drop-off, follow the internal roads and the fire driveway around the community, then look at the central landscape between the six towers and how it runs down to the lake edge. The aerial view above shows that arrangement.

SNN Kudlu towers on the plan

The six towers stand around the central landscape. Four of them, Towers A to D, rise to two basements, a ground floor and 24 upper floors. The other two, Towers E and F, rise to two basements, a ground floor and 22. Together they hold 1,917 homes: 160 2 BHK, 1,333 3 BHK, 338 4.5 BHK compact and 86 4.5 BHK large.

Because the towers are grouped around a shared green centre, a home can look over the gardens, the lake or a neighbouring tower, depending on its position. Which towers face the lake, and which formats each tower holds, will be clear from the detailed plans at launch.

Arrival, roads and basements at SNN Kudlu

Residents and visitors come in through an arrival court with a drop-off; the gallery shows it tree-lined, with a gatehouse. From there, internal roads and basement access lead to the two basement levels below the community.

In plans like this, the basements take most parked cars off the ground, leaving it free for landscape. A fire driveway runs around the SNN Kudlu community, kept clear so that fire engines can reach the towers. Ask at launch how basement parking will be allotted to each home.

The central landscape and the lake edge at SNN Kudlu

About 10.9 acres of the SNN Kudlu site is landscape. On the plan, the central landscape fills the space between the towers and continues to the lake edge, with parks and open spaces, lawns, shrubs and planted corridors.

Most of the amenities named so far are outdoor ones: the herb and sensory garden, the fruit orchard, the treetop trail, the wild-watching deck, the sports lawns and the play areas. The gallery also shows a rain garden with a stone path and a planted stream bed.

Civic amenity land and shared spaces at SNN Kudlu

The SNN Kudlu plan sets aside civic amenity land. In a large layout, this is land kept for community uses such as a school, a clinic or other public facilities. What it will hold at SNN Kudlu is not yet known; ask at launch.

The clubhouse is the other main shared building. Together with the parks, lawns, play areas and basements, it forms the common areas that every one of the 1,917 homes will help maintain, which is worth remembering when you compare maintenance charges at launch.
