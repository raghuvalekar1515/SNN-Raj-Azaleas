SNN Kudlu Project Overview

SNN Kudlu is SNN Raj Corp’s community beside a lake off Hosa Road, near Silver County Road, between Haralur Road and Kudlu Gate in South-East Bengaluru. Across about 28.7 acres it brings 1,917 apartments in six towers, four rising to two basements, ground and 24 floors and two to ground and 22, with expansive balconies, lake views and abundant natural light. Choose from 2 BHK, 3 BHK and two 4.5 BHK formats, with 60+ amenities, a clubhouse and about 10.9 acres of landscape.

SNN Kudlu in brief: what is planned

SNN Kudlu is a lakefront apartment community planned by SNN Raj Corp on about 28.7 acres off Hosa Road, near Silver County Road. It sits between Haralur Road and Kudlu Gate in South-East Bengaluru, in the Haralur and Kudlu pocket also known as HSR Extension. The plan brings 1,917 homes in six towers, arranged around a central landscape that runs down to the lake edge.

You may also hear the project called SNN Raj Etternia Phase 2. Both names refer to this community, so keep both in mind when you search or compare listings. The details on this website are as of 17 October 2026, when SNN Kudlu was in pre-launch with prices on request.

SNN Kudlu towers and floors

The six towers come in two heights. Towers A to D rise to two basements, a ground floor and 24 upper floors, written as 2B + G + 24. Towers E and F rise to two basements, a ground floor and 22 upper floors, or 2B + G + 22. Below ground, the two basement levels are reached from the internal roads, and at ground level an arrival court and drop-off welcome residents and visitors.

For a buyer, the tower and floor shape daily life as much as the home itself. In a community of 1,917 homes, ask at launch how many homes share each floor and each lift, which towers look over the lake, and whether upper floors in Towers A to D carry a floor-rise charge. Those answers will come with the floor plans and the cost sheet.

SNN Kudlu homes: four formats

SNN Kudlu offers four home formats: 160 2 BHK homes, 1,333 3 BHK homes, 338 4.5 BHK compact homes and 86 4.5 BHK large homes. A 4.5 BHK here means four bedrooms plus a flexible extra room, in either a compact or a larger plan. The 3 BHK makes up most of the community, so it is the format with the most homes to choose from when sales open.

Carpet and super built-up areas for each format will be shared at launch. Until then, the indicative 3D layouts on the SNN Kudlu floor plans page show how the rooms in each home are arranged.

SNN Kudlu landscape, lake and open spaces

About 10.9 acres of SNN Kudlu is planned as landscape: parks and open spaces, lawns, shrubs and planted corridors, with the central landscape between the towers reaching the lake edge. The gallery shows a tree-lined arrival court, a rain garden with a stone path and a planted stream bed, and lawns with shade trees between the towers.

The landscape also holds many of the amenities named so far, from the herb and sensory garden and the fruit orchard to the treetop trail, the wild-watching deck and the sports lawns. The SNN Kudlu amenities page goes through each of them.

SNN Kudlu location in short

The site is off Hosa Road near Silver County Road, close to HSR Layout. Haralur Road is about 3.1 km away and Kudlu Gate about 4.4 km. For daily travel, the Hosa Road metro station is about 5.4 km away and Silk Board Junction about 7.9 km, while RGA Tech Park is about 8.8 km and Wipro SEZ on Sarjapur Road about 9.4 km. Manipal Hospital on Sarjapur Road is about 5.5 km away and Market Square Mall about 4.9 km.

These are approximate driving distances. The SNN Kudlu location page has the full list of landmarks and a map link for live travel times.
