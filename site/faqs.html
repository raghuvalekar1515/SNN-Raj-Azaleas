SNN Kudlu: about the project

What is SNN Kudlu?

SNN Kudlu is a new community by SNN Raj Corp: 1,917 lakefront 2, 3 and 4.5 BHK apartments in six towers of up to 24 floors on about 28.7 acres, off Hosa Road between Haralur Road and Kudlu Gate. It is in pre-launch, so register now for priority access at launch.

Why is it called SNN Kudlu?

It is named after its location near Kudlu Gate in South-East Bengaluru. SNN Kudlu is also known as SNN Raj Etternia Phase 2.

How large is the SNN Kudlu site?

The SNN Kudlu site covers about 28.7 acres beside a lake, off Hosa Road. On it, SNN Raj Corp plans 1,917 apartments in six towers, a clubhouse and about 10.9 acres of landscape: parks, lawns, shrubs and planted corridors that run from the central landscape between the towers down to the lake edge. Two basement levels sit below the community, and part of the land is kept as civic amenity land.

How many towers will SNN Kudlu have, and how tall are they?

Six. Towers A, B, C and D are planned with two basement levels, a ground floor and 24 upper floors (2B + G + 24). Towers E and F are a little lower, with two basement levels, a ground floor and 22 upper floors (2B + G + 22). The six stand around the central landscape, so the tower and the floor you pick will shape what your balconies look out on.

What stage is SNN Kudlu at today?

Pre-launch. SNN Raj Corp has set out the plan, 1,917 homes in four formats across six towers on about 28.7 acres, but has not yet released prices, home areas or a payment plan; those come at launch. Everything on this website reflects the project as of 17 October 2026. Registering your interest now means the price sheet and floor plans reach you as soon as they are out, with priority access when sales open.

Are the SNN Kudlu master plan and pictures final?

Not yet. The master plan, the gallery views and the 3D home layouts are indicative. Four of the smaller amenity pictures were taken at another SNN Raj Corp apartment community, as a guide to what is planned. Until the sanctioned plan and the launch documents are out, treat layouts, areas, amenities and timelines as subject to change, and base your decision on the detailed plans shared at launch.

SNN Kudlu homes, sizes and plans

What configurations are available?

2 BHK (160 homes), 3 BHK (1,333 homes) and two 4.5 BHK formats, compact (338 homes) and large (86 homes), each 4.5 BHK with four bedrooms and a flexible extra room.

What does 4.5 BHK mean at SNN Kudlu?

The “.5” stands for a flexible extra room that comes on top of four bedrooms. Both SNN Kudlu 4.5 BHK plans, the compact and the large, include it. How you use it is up to you: a work-from-home office, a room for visiting parents, a reading room or a space for the children's toys. Its exact size will appear on the 2D floor plans released at launch.

How do the 4.5 BHK compact and 4.5 BHK large differ?

Both have four bedrooms, the extra room, a kitchen and balconies. In the indicative layouts, the compact plan puts living and dining together in one open room, while the large plan gives dining a room of its own beside a separate living room. With 86 large homes against 338 compact ones, the large plan is the scarcest format. Their areas, and so the price gap between them, will be known at launch.

What will the carpet and super built-up areas be?

Not yet known: SNN Raj Corp will publish the areas of all four formats at launch. Expect two figures for every home. The carpet area measures only the space inside your own walls, where your furniture goes. The super built-up area is larger, because it adds your share of common spaces such as lobbies, staircases and the clubhouse. Compare homes on carpet area, and ask what loading the super built-up figure carries.

Will SNN Kudlu homes have balconies?

Yes. In the indicative 3D layouts, the 2 BHK has a balcony, and the 3 BHK and both 4.5 BHK plans have balconies. The project is planned with expansive balconies, lake views and plenty of natural light. How many balconies each home has, their sizes and which rooms open onto them will be clear from the 2D plans at launch, so check them for the exact home you choose.

Which SNN Kudlu homes will face the lake?

That depends on the tower and on where a home sits on its floor. The six towers are arranged around the central landscape, which runs down to the lake edge, so some homes will look over the water and others over the gardens or another tower. The tower-wise plans released at launch will show the facing of each home. If a lake view matters, say so when you register.

How can I get the SNN Kudlu floor plans?

The floor plans page already shows indicative 3D layouts of all four formats: 2 BHK, 3 BHK, 4.5 BHK compact and 4.5 BHK large. The detailed 2D plans, with room sizes and areas, will be shared at launch. Register through the form and the sales team will send them to you as soon as they are released, along with the price sheet.

What is the price of SNN Kudlu?

Prices will be announced at launch. Register your interest to receive the price list first, with priority access to the home you prefer.

Why has the SNN Kudlu price not been published?

Because the project is still in pre-launch. A developer usually fixes the rate per square foot, the area of each format and the extra charges together, and SNN Raj Corp will announce all of them at launch. Any figure quoted before then is a guess, not a price. Registering your interest is the quickest way to receive the SNN Kudlu price sheet the moment it is released.

What will the SNN Kudlu cost sheet include?

Each SNN Kudlu home will get its own cost sheet once sales open. Expect it to show the carpet and super built-up areas, the base price, any floor-rise or view premium, car parking if it is charged separately, clubhouse or amenity charges, maintenance deposits, the applicable taxes, and stamp duty and registration, which are extra. Ask for one for every home on your shortlist and compare the totals.

Are stamp duty and registration included in the SNN Kudlu price?

No. Stamp duty and registration are paid on top of the price of an SNN Kudlu home. They fall due when the sale deed is registered, and a home loan does not usually cover them, so plan to pay them from your own savings. The cost sheet for your chosen home is the place to see what to budget for.

What payment plan will SNN Kudlu offer?

SNN Raj Corp will share the payment plan with the price list at launch. A construction-linked plan ties each instalment to a stage of work on the building, so payments are spread over the construction period, while some projects also offer plans with a larger share paid early. When the SNN Kudlu plan is out, ask how its stages are defined and whether they track your own tower.

Can I take a home loan for an SNN Kudlu apartment?

Yes, a home loan is the usual way to fund an apartment like this; ask at launch which lenders the project works with. For an under-construction home, the bank releases the loan in stages that follow the payment plan. Since prices are not out yet, use the pre-launch period to check your loan eligibility, gather your income papers and set aside your own share, so you can move quickly when the price sheet arrives.

What should I check before paying a token amount?

Get the cost sheet and the 2D floor plan for the exact home, with its tower, floor and facing. Read the payment plan and the terms on which the token is refunded if you do not go ahead. Make sure the developer named on the paperwork is SNN Raj Corp and that the payment goes to the developer's own account, by cheque or bank transfer, not to an agent. This website never collects money.

SNN Kudlu amenities and daily life

What amenities are planned?

60+ amenities around a clubhouse and about 10.9 acres of landscape by the lake, including a herb and sensory garden, a fruit orchard, a treetop trail, a wild-watching deck, a rock climbing wall, outdoor fitness, a floating cabana, cricket and football lawns, a multi-functional lawn and play areas for children, toddlers and pets.

What will the SNN Kudlu clubhouse have?

SNN Kudlu will have a clubhouse, pictured in the gallery with a stone terrace and steps at sunset, but its contents are not yet public. Expect the clubhouse plan, with its size and facilities, in the launch brochure. With 1,917 homes sharing it, ask how big the clubhouse will be, whether it will be ready when you move in, and whether residents pay a separate membership or usage charge.

Is there a swimming pool or a gym at SNN Kudlu?

Neither has been named so far. The amenities listed for SNN Kudlu are mostly outdoors: the trails and gardens, the sports lawns, outdoor fitness, a rock climbing wall and play areas. The full list of 60+ amenities and the clubhouse plan will come with the launch brochure, so if a pool or an indoor gym matters to you, ask for both before you choose a home.

What is planned for children and pets at SNN Kudlu?

Three play spaces are named: a children's play area, a toddler zone for the very young and a pet play zone for dogs. Beyond them, the cricket, football and multi-functional lawns give older children room for games, and the treetop trail and the wild-watching deck suit nature walks together. When the master plan is detailed at launch, check how far each of these is from your tower.

How will water be managed at SNN Kudlu?

Three systems are planned: rainwater harvesting, with collection tanks and recharge pits; water treatment and filtration; and reuse pipework, so that water can be used again. With about 10.9 acres of landscape to keep green, ask at launch how much of the garden water will come from harvested and reused water, and how the running of these systems will be paid for.

How will parking work at SNN Kudlu?

Parking is planned in two basement levels below the community, reached from the internal roads through basement access. Visitors arrive at an arrival court with a drop-off. How many parking spaces come with each home, whether they are included in the price or charged separately, and which basement serves your tower will be set out at launch, so look for them on the cost sheet.

SNN Kudlu developer: SNN Raj Corp

Who is the developer?

SNN Raj Corp, formerly SNN Builders, building homes in Bengaluru since 1994. SNN Builders Private Limited was incorporated in 2009 as part of the SNN Group, which by May 2016 had completed more than 40 projects with about 8.0 million sq ft of saleable area, mainly in South and South-East Bengaluru. Its Bengaluru homes include SNN Clermont, SNN Raj Etternia and Raj Bay Vista.

When was SNN Raj Corp founded, and who leads it?

SNN Raj Corp traces its start to 1994 and builds in Bengaluru. Its founders are at the helm: Shah Sanjay as Founder and Managing Director, and Shivashankar Bhat as Founder and Director. It builds apartments and villas, and was earlier known as SNN Builders. More on its history is on the About SNN Raj Corp page.

Which projects has SNN Raj Corp completed?

Its completed communities include SNN Raj Etternia on Haralur Road, SNN Raj Serenity in Begur, SNN Raj Greenbay in Electronic City Phase 2, SNN Raj Lakeview near BTM Layout and Madiwala Lake, SNN Raj Spiritua in JP Nagar and SNN Clermont in Hebbal. For a buyer at SNN Kudlu, SNN Raj Etternia is the natural one to visit, since SNN Kudlu also goes by SNN Raj Etternia Phase 2.

Which projects is SNN Raj Corp building now?

As of 25 November 2026, three SNN Raj Corp projects were ongoing: Raj Bay Vista on Kodichikkanahalli Main Road in Bilekahalli, Raj Viviente on Bannerghatta Road and Raj High Gardens on the Chandapura and Hosur Road side. Raj Elegante on the Hebbal Corridor and Raj Boulevard in Electronic City Phase 1 were listed as upcoming. A walk around one of the ongoing sites is a practical way to judge current construction before you commit.

Is SNN Raj Corp the same company as SNN Estates?

No. SNN Raj Corp and SNN Estates are separate companies, with their own leaders, offices and websites, even though each describes itself as formerly SNN Builders and both list projects from the SNN Builders years. SNN Kudlu is an SNN Raj Corp project. When bookings open, check that the agreement and every receipt name SNN Raj Corp as the developer.
