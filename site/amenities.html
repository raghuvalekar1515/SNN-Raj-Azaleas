SNN Kudlu Amenities

SNN Kudlu plans 60+ amenities across wellness, play and social spaces, around a clubhouse and about 10.9 acres of landscape by the lake: a herb and sensory garden, a fruit orchard, a treetop trail and a wild-watching deck, a rock climbing wall, outdoor fitness and a floating cabana, cricket and football lawns, a multi-functional lawn, and play areas for children, toddlers and pets.
