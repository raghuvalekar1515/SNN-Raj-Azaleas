Nature trails and gardens at SNN Kudlu

The landscape is the centre of the SNN Kudlu plan. A treetop trail and a wild-watching deck are planned for slow walks and quiet time outdoors, while the herb and sensory garden and the fruit orchard bring planting you can see, touch and smell up close.

Around them, the landscape plan has parks and open spaces, lawns, shrubs and planted corridors. The gallery shows a rain garden with a stone path and a planted stream bed, and a central landscape of lawns and shade trees between the towers. For a family that wants green space at the door rather than a drive away, this landscape is the part of the plan to study most closely.

Sports and fitness at SNN Kudlu

The sports amenities named so far are outdoors: separate cricket and football lawns, a multi-functional lawn, an outdoor fitness area and a rock climbing wall. Having separate lawns for cricket and football means a match on one need not take over the other.

A gym, indoor courts and a swimming pool are often found in a clubhouse, but they are not on the SNN Kudlu list published so far. If any of them matter to you, ask for the clubhouse plan at launch.

The SNN Kudlu clubhouse and social spaces

The clubhouse is the main shared building at SNN Kudlu. The gallery shows it with a stone terrace and steps at sunset. Its floor area, rooms and facilities will be confirmed with the brochure at launch.

A floating cabana adds an outdoor place to sit out. With 1,917 homes across six towers, ask at launch how the clubhouse and the cabana will be booked for private events, and whether any facility carries a separate charge.

Play spaces for children and pets at SNN Kudlu

SNN Kudlu plans a children’s play area and a separate toddler zone, so the youngest children have a space of their own. A pet play zone gives dogs room to run without sharing the children’s spaces.

For older children, the cricket, football and multi-functional lawns add open ground, and the treetop trail and the gardens turn the landscape itself into a place to explore.

Water and landscape care at SNN Kudlu

Keeping about 10.9 acres of landscape green takes water, and the SNN Kudlu infrastructure plans for it on site. Rainwater is harvested into collection tanks and recharge pits, water is treated and filtered, and reuse pipework is part of the plan.

For residents, these systems matter as much as any amenity, because they shape how the community is run day to day. At launch, ask how harvested and reused water will serve the gardens, and how the upkeep of the landscape and amenities will be charged.
