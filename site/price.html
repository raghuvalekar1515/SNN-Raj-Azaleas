What will decide the price of each SNN Kudlu home

Apartment prices are usually quoted as a rate per square foot of super built-up area, multiplied by the area of the home, with other charges listed separately. At SNN Kudlu, ask how each of these is priced when the rate card comes out:

What the SNN Kudlu cost sheet will list

A cost sheet is the full price statement for one specific home. It names the tower, the floor and the home number, gives the carpet and super built-up areas, and lists every charge that makes up the total. At SNN Kudlu, with 1,917 homes across six towers, ask for a cost sheet for each home you shortlist rather than a starting price for the format. A typical cost sheet shows:

  • the base price, which is the rate multiplied by the area;
  • any floor rise or location charges;
  • car parking, where it is charged separately;
  • clubhouse and amenity charges, if any;
  • maintenance and deposits collected at handover;
  • the taxes that apply to the home;
  • stamp duty and registration, which are extra.

Compare totals, not base rates. Two SNN Kudlu homes with the same rate can end up with different totals once parking, floor rise and other charges are added.

Carpet area and super built-up area at SNN Kudlu

Carpet area is the usable floor area inside a home's walls: the bedrooms, the living and dining space, the kitchen and the toilets. Super built-up area adds a share of the walls, the lift lobbies and staircases, and the common areas such as the clubhouse. Because rates are often quoted on super built-up area, the gap between the two figures affects what you pay for each square foot you actually use.

SNN Kudlu's areas will be published at launch. When they are, compare the carpet areas of the 4.5 BHK compact and the 4.5 BHK large closely. Both have four bedrooms and an extra room, and the large plan adds a separate dining room in its indicative layout, so the carpet area shows how much more space the higher price buys.

SNN Kudlu payment plan at launch

The SNN Kudlu payment plan will be shared with the price list at launch. Many under-construction projects use a construction-linked plan: a booking amount first, then instalments that fall due as the building reaches set stages, such as the foundation, groups of floor slabs, finishing work and handover.

For towers that rise to 24 upper floors, a plan like this spreads your payments over the building period instead of asking for most of the price up front. When the SNN Kudlu plan is released, check whether each instalment is tied to the progress of your own tower, and how you will be told that a stage has been reached.

Home loans for an SNN Kudlu apartment

Most buyers fund an apartment with a home loan. The lender looks at your income and repayment record, decides how much it will lend against the home, and leaves the rest as your own contribution. For an under-construction home, the loan is usually released in parts that match the stages of the payment plan, so interest builds up only on the amount released so far.

With SNN Kudlu in pre-launch, you have time to prepare. Check your eligibility now, so that when the price sheet for your chosen format arrives you already know what you can borrow. Keep your own funds ready for the charges outside the base price, including stamp duty.
