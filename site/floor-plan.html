SNN Kudlu 2 BHK

The 2 BHK is the SNN Kudlu home with the fewest bedrooms, and one of the less common formats, with 160 of the 1,917 homes. Its indicative layout has two bedrooms, a combined living and dining room, a kitchen and a balcony.

It suits a couple or a small family who want a home between Haralur Road and Kudlu Gate in a large lakefront community. With 160 of them spread over the project, ask early which towers and floors have 2 BHK homes, and ask for the carpet area and the number of toilets when the plans are released.

SNN Kudlu 3 BHK

The 3 BHK is the core of SNN Kudlu: 1,333 homes, most of the community. The indicative layout has three bedrooms, a combined living and dining room, a kitchen and balconies, where the 2 BHK layout shows a single balcony.

Because it is by far the most numerous format, the 3 BHK is likely to give you the most homes to choose from across towers, floors and facings. It suits families who want a bedroom each for parents and children, or a third bedroom that doubles as a study.

SNN Kudlu 4.5 BHK compact

Each 4.5 BHK at SNN Kudlu has four bedrooms and a flexible extra room, which is the half in 4.5. The compact format, with 338 homes, is the smaller of the two 4.5 BHK plans. Its indicative layout shows four bedrooms, the extra room, a combined living and dining room, a kitchen and balconies.

The extra room can serve as a study, a guest room, a prayer room or a playroom, depending on how you set it up. The compact plan suits a larger family who need four bedrooms and that extra space, but want a single open living and dining area.

SNN Kudlu 4.5 BHK large

The 4.5 BHK large is the rarest home at SNN Kudlu, with 86 planned. Its indicative layout separates the living room from the dining room, alongside four bedrooms, the extra room, a kitchen and balconies. That separate dining room is the clearest difference from the compact plan in the 3D layouts.

It suits families who host often, or several generations living together who want a living room apart from the dining space. With only 86 of them among 1,917 homes, register early if this is the format you want.

How to read the SNN Kudlu floor plans

The layouts on this page are indicative 3D views of the four SNN Kudlu formats. They show the rooms and how they connect, but not the final dimensions. At launch, ask for the 2D floor plan of each home you shortlist, showing:

  • the carpet area and the super built-up area;
  • the size of each room;
  • the number of toilets and where they are;
  • the balconies, and which rooms open onto them;
  • the direction the main door and the balconies face.

Carpet area is the space you can use inside the walls; super built-up area adds a share of walls, lobbies and common areas. Between the 2 BHK, the 3 BHK and the two 4.5 BHK plans, compare room by room on carpet area rather than on the total alone.

Choosing a tower and floor at SNN Kudlu

The floor plan is half the choice; the tower and floor are the other half. Towers A to D rise to two basements, a ground floor and 24 upper floors, and Towers E and F to two basements, a ground floor and 22. Because the towers stand around a central landscape beside the lake, a home's position decides whether its balconies look over the lake, the gardens or a neighbouring tower.

When the plans are out, ask which towers hold each format, which homes face the lake and how many homes share each floor. Those answers matter as much as the room sizes.
