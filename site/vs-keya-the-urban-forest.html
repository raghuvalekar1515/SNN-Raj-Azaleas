SNN Kudlu vs Keya The Urban Forest

Two apartment communities in East Bengaluru, at different stages: SNN Kudlu, in pre-launch off Hosa Road between Haralur Road and Kudlu Gate, and Keya The Urban Forest, under construction on Kasavanahalli Main Road, off Sarjapur Road. This guide sets them side by side so you can see which one fits your plans.

Location: off Hosa Road or off Sarjapur Road

SNN Kudlu is off Hosa Road near Silver County Road, between Haralur Road, about 3.1 km away, and Kudlu Gate, about 4.4 km, close to HSR Layout. The Hosa Road metro station is about 5.4 km away and Silk Board Junction about 7.9 km.

Keya The Urban Forest is on Kasavanahalli Main Road, off Sarjapur Road, between the Kaikondrahalli and Kasavanahalli lakes, about 3 km from the Outer Ring Road junction. Maple Bear Canadian School is about 350 m away and RMZ Ecoworld about 6.4 km.

Manipal Hospital on Sarjapur Road is about 5.5 km from SNN Kudlu and about 3.2 km from Keya. In short, SNN Kudlu sits towards Kudlu Gate and HSR Layout, and Keya towards Sarjapur Road and the Outer Ring Road.

Homes and sizes compared

SNN Kudlu has four formats: 160 2 BHK, 1,333 3 BHK, 338 4.5 BHK compact and 86 4.5 BHK large homes. Each 4.5 BHK pairs four bedrooms with a flexible extra room. Areas will be announced at launch.

Keya The Urban Forest has five types: a 2 BHK from 1,351 sq.ft, a 3 BHK from 1,710 sq.ft, a 3 BHK Grand from 1,910 sq.ft, a 4 BHK of 2,387 sq.ft and a 4 BHK Grand from 2,610 sq.ft, all super built-up.

If you need four bedrooms and an extra room, SNN Kudlu's 4.5 BHK plans are built around that. If you want to compare exact sizes today, Keya has published them; SNN Kudlu's will follow at launch.

Scale and open space

SNN Kudlu is the larger community: 1,917 homes on about 28.7 acres in six towers, with about 10.9 acres of landscape running to a lake. Keya The Urban Forest is smaller: 727 homes on 8 acres 15 guntas in four towers, with 83% open space and an elevated podium garden of more than 6 acres.

A larger community usually brings more neighbours and a wider spread of shared spaces; a smaller one brings fewer people sharing each facility. Neither is better in itself; it depends on how you like to live.

Amenities and clubhouse

SNN Kudlu plans 60+ amenities around a clubhouse, among them a herb and sensory garden, a fruit orchard, a treetop trail, a wild-watching deck, a floating cabana, a rock climbing wall, outdoor fitness, cricket, football and multi-functional lawns, and play areas for children, toddlers and pets. Its clubhouse details will come at launch.

Keya The Urban Forest has over 95 amenities. Club Verde, its 40,000 sq.ft clubhouse, has a temperature-controlled infinity pool, indoor badminton and squash courts, a gym, a yoga room and working pods, and the podium carries cycling and jogging tracks, an open-air theatre, courts for six sports, a pet park and a bio pond.

Stage, prices and timing

SNN Kudlu is in pre-launch: prices, areas and the payment plan will be announced at launch, and registering interest gives you priority access. Keya The Urban Forest is under construction, with completion planned for December 2027, and has published prices from ₹1.70 Cr for a 2 BHK, including the car park, infrastructure charges and GST, with stamp duty and registration extra.

The choice often comes down to timing. Pre-launch gives you an early pick of homes and time to arrange finance before the price is fixed; an under-construction project gives you a known price and a planned completion date now.

Who each one suits

SNN Kudlu suits you if

  • you want a home in a large lakefront community with about 10.9 acres of landscape;
  • you need four bedrooms and an extra room, in the 4.5 BHK compact or large;
  • you prefer the Haralur Road, Kudlu Gate and HSR Layout side, with the Hosa Road metro station about 5.4 km away;
  • you are happy to register at pre-launch and wait for prices and plans at launch.

Keya The Urban Forest suits you if

  • you want published prices and sizes now;
  • you want a planned completion date, December 2027;
  • you prefer a smaller community of 727 homes with a podium garden;
  • you want to be off Sarjapur Road near the Outer Ring Road junction, with Maple Bear Canadian School about 350 m away.

Visit both before you decide

Drive to both sites at the time of day you would usually travel, walk from the towers to the clubhouse and the gardens, and ask each sales team for a cost sheet for the same type of home, so the totals compare like with like. For Keya The Urban Forest, details are on its own website, keyatheurbanforest.com; for SNN Kudlu, register here for the price sheet and floor plans.

SNN Kudlu vs Keya The Urban Forest: summary

SNN Kudlu is a pre-launch community of 1,917 apartments on about 28.7 acres off Hosa Road, with 2, 3 and 4.5 BHK homes and prices at launch. Keya The Urban Forest is an under-construction community of 727 homes on 8 acres 15 guntas off Sarjapur Road, with 2, 3 and 4 BHK homes, published prices and completion planned for December 2027. Look at SNN Kudlu for its scale, its landscape and the 4.5 BHK plans; look at Keya for a smaller community with a known price and date.
